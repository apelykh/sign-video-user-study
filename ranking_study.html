Sign Language Video Generation User Study

Welcome

Thank you for your interest in this study evaluating the quality of computer-generated British Sign Language (BSL) videos.

What you will do: This survey takes approximately 15–20 minutes. You will watch sets of short generated BSL videos and answer questions about their quality — ranking the videos and rating the quality of hand shapes and facial expressions. You may replay each video as many times as you like.

Participant Consent

Purpose. This research evaluates the visual quality and realism of BSL videos produced by automatic generation models.

Eligibility. You must be 18 years of age or older to take part.

What is collected. Your rankings and quality ratings of the videos, your self-reported BSL proficiency level, and anonymous interaction data. No personal identifying information, audio, or video of you is recorded.

  • Your participation is entirely voluntary, and you may stop at any time by closing the window; partial responses may be retained.
  • All data is stored anonymously under a randomly generated participant ID and used only for academic research.
  • Results may be published in aggregate; no individual can be identified.

Questions? Contact a.pelykh@surrey.ac.uk.
