BSL Comprehension Study

Welcome

Thank you for your interest in this study on the comprehensibility of computer-generated British Sign Language (BSL) videos.

This study is open to advanced / fluent BSL users aged 18 or older. Please take part only if you can comfortably read BSL.

What you will do: You will watch a series of short generated BSL videos. For each one, type the closest English translation you can of what is being signed. Each video is shown at normal speed and at half speed — you may replay both as many times as you like. If you cannot understand a video at all, tick the box to indicate this instead of guessing.

Participant Consent

Purpose. This research evaluates how comprehensible BSL videos produced by automatic generation models are to fluent signers.

Eligibility. You must be 18 years of age or older and a fluent BSL user to take part.

What is collected. Your typed English translations and anonymous interaction data (how many times you replayed each video, time spent, and which video version you saw). No personal identifying information, audio, or video of you is recorded.

  • Your participation is entirely voluntary, and you may stop at any time by closing the window; partial responses may be retained.
  • All data is stored anonymously under a randomly generated participant ID and used only for academic research.
  • Results may be published in aggregate; no individual can be identified.

Questions? Contact a.pelykh@surrey.ac.uk.
